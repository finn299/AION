Registered Agent

Document intelligence — built into the agent service.

Every piece of mail and process served at any AION office is opened, OCR'd, classified and triaged before it reaches your inbox. The cover note tells you what it is, why it matters and what we recommend.

The 7-class document model

Our classifier was trained on more than 280,000 documents served at AION offices and supplemented with adversarial examples drawn from public PACER filings. Classes include: service of process, tax notices, annual report reminders, franchise tax demands, regulatory letters, vendor mail, and personal mail mis-directed to the agent address.

Why classification matters operationally

Service of process triggers a litigation clock; missing it can result in default judgment. Tax notices from California's FTB and New York's DTF can compound penalties at 5% per month. A misrouted W-2 from a former vendor isn't urgent. Treating these the same way wastes legal hours and lets real deadlines slip.

  • Hard SLAs on classification accuracy (98.7% on the 7-class, audited quarterly)
  • Every classification reviewed by a paralegal before alerting the client
  • Full audit log of who saw what and when, exportable for litigation
  • Custom routing rules per company (e.g., route all CA notices to outside counsel)

How the intake pipeline runs

Mail arrives at a state office, is photographed front and back, OCR'd, run through the classifier, reviewed by a paralegal, attached to your matter in the AION client portal, and announced via your configured alert channel (Slack, Teams, email, SMS). Median end-to-end time is 71 minutes during business hours.
