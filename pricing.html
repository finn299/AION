Pricing FAQ

Questions, answered up front.

Are there any hidden fees?
No. Every package and à la carte module quotes a fixed monthly or one-time fee. State filing fees and third-party costs (e.g. USPTO filing fees) pass through at cost and are itemized on every invoice.
Can I switch packages later?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any renewal point. Most clients start on Founder or Operator and graduate to Enterprise as they grow.
Is AI really doing the work, or is this just marketing?
AI is genuinely doing the drafting, classification and assembly — that is why we can price below traditional firms. Every legally consequential output (filings, returns, contracts) is reviewed and signed by a licensed human before it leaves AION.
What if I already use other providers?
We migrate. If you are switching registered agents or accountants, we handle the change-of-agent filings, file transfers and provider notifications — usually within a week and at no extra charge.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or early-stage founders?
Yes. Verified 501(c)(3) organizations and pre-revenue founders may qualify for 20% off the Founder bundle. Reach out and we will review your situation.
