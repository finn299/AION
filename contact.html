Common Questions

Before you reach out…

What happens after I submit the form?
A senior human advisor reviews your message and emails you within one business day. If a call makes sense, we will send a scheduling link. The first conversation — usually 30 minutes — is always free.
I am not in the United States. Can you still help?
Yes. A significant portion of our clients are founders outside the U.S. forming their first American entity. We handle international payments, document apostille, and time-zone-flexible scheduling.
How quickly can you start work?
For most modules, we can begin within 24–48 hours of contract signing. Same-day formation is possible in most states.
Is the consultation really free?
Yes — and there is no obligation. You will leave with a clear plan, whether or not you end up working with us.
