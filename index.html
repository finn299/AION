How we operate

Four operating principles.

We pair an AI-native execution engine with credentialed human reviewers. AI handles routine drafting and assembly; humans hold judgment, licensure and accountability.

01

AI does the work

Drafts filings, classifies transactions, prepares returns, generates marketing copy and assembles diligence — at machine speed, 24/7.

02

Humans hold the standard

Every legal filing, tax return, payroll run and IP submission is reviewed and signed by a credentialed attorney, CPA or operator.

03

Fixed-fee, never billable hour

Because AI handles the bulk of the work, we quote fixed monthly or one-time fees well below a multi-vendor stack. State fees pass through at cost.

04

One client lead, one platform

A named human owns your relationship across every module. You email one person; they coordinate AI and specialists behind the scenes.
