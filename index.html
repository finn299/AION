Human Oversight

AI speeds the work.
Humans hold the line.

AI is excellent at drafting, classifying and assembling. It is not a licensed attorney or CPA, and we do not pretend otherwise.

Every legal filing, tax return, payroll run and IP submission is reviewed and signed off by a credentialed human before it leaves AION. You always know which decisions are made by AI and which are made by a person — it is visible on every artifact.
