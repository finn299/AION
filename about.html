About AION

An AI-native firm with humans on the line.

AION LLC is a corporate services firm built around an AI platform. Our AI drafts, classifies and assembles the routine work. Licensed attorneys, CPAs and operators review and sign every output before it leaves us.

Our Mission

Make great companies easy to run.

Founders should spend their days on customers and product — not chasing a registered agent who never returns calls, or stitching together five providers to make payroll work.

AION brings every corporate function onto a single AI platform with one accountable team. One client lead. One invoice. One source of truth. We measure ourselves on a simple question: did we save you a meeting today?

How we are built

AI core, human edge.

Our platform combines large language models tuned to legal, tax and finance work with deterministic compliance checks and credentialed human reviewers. We use AI where it is reliable — and humans where judgment, licensure or accountability is required.

  • AI drafts every routine document & output
  • Humans review and sign before anything is filed
  • You see exactly what AI did and what a human did

Our Approach

The work, broken down.

i

AI as a junior associate

The AI handles the work a brand-new associate would handle — first drafts, classifications, research, calculation, formatting. It is fast, consistent and never tired.

ii

Licensed humans as senior counsel

Every legal, tax, IP and payroll output is reviewed by a credentialed professional. They edit, approve, sign and take accountability.

iii

Account lead as your front door

One named person owns your relationship across modules. You email them; they coordinate the AI and the specialists behind the scenes.

iv

You see everything

Every artifact in your portal shows what AI generated, what a human reviewed, and when. You are never wondering who did what.
