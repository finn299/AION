About AION

An AI-native firm with humans on the line.

AION LLC is a corporate services firm built around an AI platform. Our AI drafts, classifies and assembles the routine work. Licensed attorneys, CPAs and operators review and sign every output before it leaves us.

Our Mission

Make great companies easy to run.

Founders should spend their days on customers and product, not chasing five vendors to make payroll work. We bring every corporate function onto a single AI platform with one accountable team.

01

AI does the work

Drafts filings, classifies transactions, prepares returns, generates marketing copy — at machine speed, 24/7.

02

Humans hold the standard

Every legal filing, tax return, payroll run and IP submission is reviewed and signed by a credentialed professional.

03

Fixed-fee, never billable

Because AI handles the bulk of the work, we quote fixed monthly fees well below a multi-vendor stack.

04

One client lead

A named human owns your relationship across every module. You email one person; they coordinate behind the scenes.

Our Approach

How the work actually gets done.

AION pairs AI agents with licensed professionals. The AI handles drafting, classification and assembly — fast, consistent, never tired. The humans handle judgment, licensure and accountability — the decisions a model should not be making alone.

  • Plain-English intake — describe what you need, no jargon required.
  • AI agents draft in minutes — articles, returns, payroll, IP filings, marketing copy.
  • Licensed humans review and sign — attorneys, CPAs, senior operators with credentials attached.
  • Every artifact in your portal shows what AI did and what a human approved.

Built On

  • Large language models tuned for legal, tax and finance work
  • Deterministic compliance checks & deadline tracking
  • Bar-licensed attorneys & CPAs reviewing every consequential output
  • Secure document workflows & audit-trail portal
